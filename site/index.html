A wardrobe for World of Warcraft

Eclipse is a free overlay for the 3.3.5a client. Every appearance, mount, spell and title in the game, on your own character.

Download Explore features

Showcase video coming soon

Everything in one overlay

Press F2 in game to open it. The key can be changed in Settings.

Wardrobe

Every item appearance and set in the game, previewed on your character before you wear it.

Customization

Race, sex, skin, hair and face, with custom colours for skin, hair and eyes.

Mounts

Every mount, shown at full size. Browse the list or let the randomizer choose.

Creature morph

Take the form of any creature the client can render.

Enchants

Any enchant effect on either weapon, and a colour of your choice for each weapon slot.

Incarnations

Druid forms, warlock demons, hunter pets, ammunition and hearthstones.

Spell visuals

Give any of your spells or auras the visuals of another, and recolour them.

Procs

Change how an aura looks, or how a spell looks while an aura is active.

Optimize

Hide other players’ spell effects in raids for a higher frame rate. Boss mechanics are always shown.

Titles

Any title on your name, or text of your own, in the colours you choose.

Effects

3D models such as wings and capes, attached to your character and animated.

Colour

Recolour units and the world around you, and outline units by type.

World

Time of day, weather, sky, fog, draw distance and field of view.

Font

Sharp text at any resolution, and restyled floating combat text.

Loadouts

Save complete looks, share them as a code, and assign one to each specialization.

Multiplayer

Other Eclipse users see your look, and you see theirs. You choose what is shared.

Download Eclipse

Includes the retail asset pack: items, mounts, fonts, effects and spells.

Download Eclipse.zip

Google Drive · Windows · World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (12340)

  1. Extract

    Unzip Eclipse.zip anywhere.

  2. Copy

    Place dinput8.dll and the Eclipse folder next to Wow.exe.

  3. Play

    Start the game, enter the world and press F2.
